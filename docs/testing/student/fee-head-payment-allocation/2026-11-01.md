Head charges are allocated by ascending snapshotted component master `DisplayOrder`, with stable head-ID tie-break; late fee receives the remaining amount after fee heads. Existing snapshots with a null priority or a mismatch between prior installment paid and tracked allocations fail closed for new head-level posting. A legacy installment with no snapshot continues its existing payment flow; no historical allocations are inferred and its breakdown is marked incomplete.
