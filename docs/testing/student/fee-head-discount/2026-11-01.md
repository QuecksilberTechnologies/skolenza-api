# Fee-head discount eligibility — 2026-11-01

## Scope

WIP, source not deployed. A school can mark a recurring fee-structure item as
eligible or ineligible for a scheme discount. Omitted values preserve the old
recurring default; one-time charges remain excluded. The quote snapshots the
effective decision and distributes rounded discounts without negative head
amounts. An item omitted from a structure is not charged by that structure.
