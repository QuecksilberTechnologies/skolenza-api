## Change behavior and persistence

The targeted scripts migrate matching persisted role/tenant-operation grants from
obsolete bulk modules to their corresponding functional module when that grant
does not already exist. They then delete obsolete references from
`HostRoleModuleAndPermission`, `RoleModuleAndPermission`,
`TenantEnabledOperation`, `TenantEnabledModule`, `PlanModuleMapping` and
`ModuleOperationMapping` before deleting obsolete Module rows.

The legacy `BULKUPLOAD` parent had five real functional modules attached in the
target database. The tenant seed restored `EMP_LIST` under `EMP_MGMT` and
Department, Designation, Role and EmployeeType under `TENANT_MGMT` before deleting
the obsolete parent. No existing functional Module PageName or OperationName was
changed.
