## Investigation and changes

- Observed failure: Visual Studio broke at `PeriodicTimer.WaitForNextTickAsync(stoppingToken)` with a user-unhandled `OperationCanceledException`.
- Confirmed cause and evidence: the timer wait was the `while` condition outside the existing inner cancellation handler, so cancellation during the idle wait escaped `ExecuteAsync`.
- Changed files and commit: `Skolenza.infrastructure/BackgroundJob/BulkImportWorker.cs` and `Skolenza.automationtests/Unit/BulkImportWorkerTests.cs`; commit pending at report creation.
