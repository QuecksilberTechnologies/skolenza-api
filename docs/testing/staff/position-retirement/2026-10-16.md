## Investigation and changes

- Observed failure: current job identity was split between `Designation` and `StaffPositionMaster`; inactive or missing mappings could leak stale titles into staff, policy and module flows.
- Confirmed cause and evidence: `StaffMember`, manager mapping, policy applicability and Position master retained legacy foreign keys, while newer professional-structure flows already used Position.
- Changed files and commit: entity/EF/API/Angular consumers, registration seed, policy applicability, bulk import guards, module seeds, transactional migration and rollback preparation. Commit is pending.
- Limits of the executed verification: deployed API/UI acceptance, actual stage 2 drop and post-deployment screenshots have not run. Local checks are not recorded as deployed acceptance.
