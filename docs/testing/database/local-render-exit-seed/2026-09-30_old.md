## Investigation and changes

- Render could not provide a current dump because the workspace was suspended
  for non-payment and closed PostgreSQL SSL connections.
- The 2026-09-28 verified dump was therefore restored locally. This does not
  claim to include Render changes made after that backup.
- Existing geography was already complete for four detailed countries. The
  current seed added the remaining 245 ISO countries and idempotently reconciled
  detailed geography.
- Regulatory truncation cascades were accepted only after direct pre-checks
  confirmed the affected dependent tables contained zero rows.
- No application source or seed business logic was changed; the ISO guard and
  sequence synchronization were execution-only safety steps.
