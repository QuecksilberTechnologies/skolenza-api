## Investigation and changes

- Confirmed gaps: no approval endpoints; no arrangement ownership/window check; no location assignment
  or policy validation; active Pending/Approved overrides could overlap.
- Change: separate Approve and Reject permission routes, mandatory rejection reason, approval-time
  revalidation, encoded employee responses, inclusive overlap validation and database race protection.
- Base arrangement overlap is intentional. Override-to-override overlap is rejected because two
  effective temporary modes for one employee/date are ambiguous.
